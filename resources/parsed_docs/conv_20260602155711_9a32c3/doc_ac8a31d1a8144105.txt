范文：Nowadays, artificial intelligence （人工智能） permeates （渗透） every aspect of campus life and brings unprecedented convenience to students’ study. While enjoying the benefits brought by advanced technology, we are also confronted with （面临） a hidden crisis: the loss of independent thinking.

Al serves as a powerful helper for modern learners. It can quickly sort out complicated knowledge points, polish compositions and answer academic puzzles anytime. However, blind reliance （盲目依赖） on Al has become prevalent among college students. Some students rely on AI to finish assignments directly instead of thinking independently. Gradually, they will lose the ability to analyze problems and form personal views, which is harmful to long-term academic growth.

From my perspective, technological tools should never replace human thinking. We ought to take advantage of AI's convenience to assist our study, but keep our own thinking initiative all the time. Only by balancing technological assistance and independent thinking can we make steady progress amid the technological wave.

中文译文

如今，人工智能渗透到校园生活的方方面面，为学生的学习带来了前所未有的便利。在享受先进科技带来红利的同时，我们也面临着一场隐性危机：独立思考能力的缺失。

人工智能是现代学习者得力的助手，它可以随时帮我们梳理复杂知识点、润色作文、解答学术难题。但当下，大学生盲目依赖Al的现象愈发普遍。部分学生直接借助人工智能完成作业，放弃自主思考。久而久之，他们会丧失分析问题、形成个人观点的能力，这对长期学业发展十分不利。

在我看来，科技工具永远不能取代人类的思考。我们可以利用人工智能的便利辅助学习，但始终要守住自身思考的主动性。只有平衡好科技助力与独立思考，我们才能在科技浪潮中稳步前行。
